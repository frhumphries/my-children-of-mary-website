Devotions

Act of Consecration

O Mary, conceived without sin, wishing this day to place myself under Thy protection, I, a child of Mary, do hereby consecrate myself entirely to Thee. I offer Thee my body, my soul, my mind, my heart, and all that I am. Take me under Thy mantle and keep me ever close to Thy Immaculate Heart.

Daily Prayer

Members are encouraged to pray the Rosary daily and to renew their consecration frequently.
