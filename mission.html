Our Mission

The mission of the Children of Mary is to foster extraordinary devotion for the Blessed Mother among the young.

The program is designed to introduce the youth of today to the beauty of total consecration to Jesus through Mary. Through age-appropriate formation, prayer, and community, we help young people develop a personal relationship with Our Lady that will last a lifetime.

"The program aims to introduce St. Philomena, the power of her intercession and the integrity of her example to the youth of the 21st century."
