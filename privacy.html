Privacy

Information submitted through this website is used only to respond to inquiries and administer membership in the Congregation of the Children of Mary.

Registration information may include a child’s name and date of birth, along with parent or guardian contact information. A parent or legal guardian must submit the form. We do not sell submitted information.

To ask about, correct, or request deletion of submitted information, email frpartain@mychildrenofmary.com.
