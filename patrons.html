Our Patrons

St. Philomena

Philomena, a young virgin martyr, who gave her life in witness to Christ at the age of 13. She is the patron saint of youth and miracles.

St. Philomena is the principal patron of the Children of Mary. Her powerful intercession and heroic purity inspire the young members of the congregation.

Our Lady, the Immaculate Conception

The Blessed Virgin Mary is the heart and soul of our congregation. All members strive to live under her mantle through total consecration.
